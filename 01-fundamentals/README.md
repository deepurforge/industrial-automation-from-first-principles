# 01 — Fundamentals

This section establishes the physical and mathematical foundations required to understand industrial automation.

## Topics

- Electricity
- Electric charge
- Voltage
- Current
- Resistance
- Power
- Energy
- AC and DC
- Electromagnetism
- Electrical signals
- Analog and digital signals
- Measurement fundamentals

## Research Approach

Each topic will be studied from:

**Physical phenomenon → Mathematical model → Electrical behavior → Measurement → Industrial application**

The objective is to understand the underlying mechanism rather than memorize definitions.
